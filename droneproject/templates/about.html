About the Detection System

Why acoustic drone detection matters — and how this system works

⚠️ Drone Threat Landscape

The Growing Need for
Acoustic Drone Detection

Commercial and DIY drones are increasingly being used for surveillance, smuggling, and security breaches. Traditional radar systems are expensive and often fail to detect small, low-flying UAVs. Acoustic detection provides a cost-effective, passive, and always-on alternative that works even in GPS-denied environments.

The pipeline processes 3-second audio clips, extracts an 85-dimensional feature vector (40 MFCC means + 40 MFCC stdevs + spectral centroid + rolloff + bandwidth + ZCR + RMS), then feeds it to a 300-tree Random Forest ensemble. A 15% probability threshold is used to maximize drone recall while keeping false alarms manageable.
Acoustic Features (85 Dimensions)
🎵
MFCCs — 80 dimensions (40 mean + 40 std)
Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients capture the timbral texture of sound. Drone rotors produce a distinctive harmonic pattern in MFCC space that differs from wind, voices, and traffic noise.
📡
Spectral Centroid — 1 dimension
The "center of mass" of the spectrum. Drone propellers generate consistent mid-to-high frequency energy, giving a characteristic centroid fingerprint.
📉
Spectral Rolloff — 1 dimension
The frequency below which 85% of spectral energy is concentrated. Helps distinguish drone buzz from broadband noise sources.
📏
Spectral Bandwidth — 1 dimension
Width of the dominant frequency band. Drones produce narrow-band periodic noise from rotor spinning, while most background noise is wideband.
〰️
Zero Crossing Rate + RMS Energy — 2 dimensions
ZCR measures signal oscillation frequency; RMS captures loudness. Together they help filter silent or highly irregular signals that cannot be drones.

Why This Approach Works
✅ High Recall Priority
Security systems must never miss a drone. The 5:1 class weight and 15% threshold achieve 97% drone recall — only 9 drones missed in 333 test samples.
✅ Passive & Real-Time
The system only listens — no radio emissions, no radar pulses. Detection takes under 2 seconds per 3-second audio clip on consumer hardware.
✅ Trained on Real Data
11,704 real-world audio clips including multiple drone models and diverse background environments (urban, rural, indoor, outdoor).
✅ Interpretable Model
Random Forest provides probability scores, feature importances, and is auditable — unlike black-box neural networks.
